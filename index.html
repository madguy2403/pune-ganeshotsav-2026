About Us

Born from a simple idea — Pune Ganeshotsav 2026 was made to make Pune's iconic Ganpati festival easier to explore.

Lokmanya Tilak transformed Ganesh Utsav into a movement that brought people together, strengthened national pride, and inspired the spirit of freedom.

Continuing that legacy, Kunal Tilak — Corporator from Ward 25-D, BJP Maharashtra spokesperson, and PMC Education Committee member — brings the festival closer to everyone through a simple, free guide to discover mandals, navigate crowds, find iconic food spots and ample parking, access emergency services, and experience Pune's Ganeshotsav with ease.

From Lokmanya Tilak's vision of Ganeshotsav as a festival that united people, to Kunal Tilak's vision of nurturing the same legacy to keep the rich culture alive — bringing people together, keeping alive the spirit of unity that made Ganeshotsav a movement.
